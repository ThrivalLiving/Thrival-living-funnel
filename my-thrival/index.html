Good to know

Wondering if this is for you?

Do I need to be fit or flexible?

No. You can choose gentle movements, take your time and adapt your experience. Start where you are; there's no need to keep up with anyone else.

What if I don't have much time?

Choose a short movement when it suits you. There's no fixed schedule and nothing to catch up on.

Will I need resistance bands?

Not for all sessions. Relax & Flow includes no-equipment options. Some supported-strength explorations use bands and a secure anchor; we'll provide recommended equipment and setup guidance.

Is this personal coaching?

My Thrival is an independent movement membership with a supportive community and monthly live session. Individual coaching is available separately through My Thrival Personal.
