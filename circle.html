Is The Circle for me?

You don't need to be fit.
You don't need great balance.
You don't need to be flexible.
You don't need any experience.

Already walking, swimming, doing Pilates, yoga or something else you love? Keep doing it. The Circle isn't here to replace the movement already in your life.

And if technology isn't your thing, that's okay too. Open The Circle. Choose your movement. Press play. If you get stuck, Brennan is there to help.

This isn't about becoming someone different.

That's why I created The Circle. Not to give you another exercise program to complete. But to give you somewhere to keep moving, learning, building confidence and discovering what's still possible — alongside a community doing it with you.
