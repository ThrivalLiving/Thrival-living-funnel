Common questions

A few things you might be wondering.

What if I'm not sure what I want to work on?

That's perfectly fine. Our first conversation is about discovering what matters to you. You might want to feel stronger, move more freely, improve your balance, explore new movement skills, or simply find a way of moving that you enjoy. We'll work out where to begin together.

How personalised is my program?

Very. We'll begin with a private Zoom conversation and movement review. From there, I'll create your own space inside Thrival, with a movement plan shaped around your goals and current abilities. I'll record videos specifically for you, use relevant demonstrations from my library, and adjust your program as we work together.

What happens during our weekly coaching calls?

This is our time to work together. We might explore a movement you're finding difficult, introduce a new challenge, refine something you've been practising, or simply see how you're progressing. Each session is guided by what you need at that point.

Do I need any equipment?

We can begin with your body, some space to move and everyday items around your home. I also use resistance bands because they create some wonderful possibilities for assistance, resistance and exploration. If they'd benefit your program, I'll help you choose the right ones. Equipment is purchased separately.

Is this suitable for my age or experience?

I work with adults of different ages, backgrounds and movement experiences. Whether you're returning to movement, looking for something beyond conventional exercise, or wanting to develop new skills, we'll explore an approach appropriate for you. We'll also discuss any injuries or health concerns before deciding whether online coaching is suitable.

What happens after the six weeks?

You'll finish with a clearer understanding of your movement, what you've been working towards and how to continue. We'll review your progress together and discuss your next steps. You can also choose to continue exploring movement through My THRIVAL.

What if I need to miss a coaching session?

If you need to miss a session, please let me know as early as possible. I'll do my best to find another suitable time, although rescheduling can't be guaranteed. If we can't arrange a replacement, that week's session is forfeited. If I ever need to cancel a session, I'll make every effort to reschedule it, even if that means meeting after your six-week program has finished.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. You can pay the $797 in full or choose two scheduled instalments of $398.50. Instalments are collected automatically. If a payment fails, Stripe may retry it, and continued access depends on the payment plan remaining active. If the plan ultimately lapses, access may be removed. I'll help you understand your options if that happens.
